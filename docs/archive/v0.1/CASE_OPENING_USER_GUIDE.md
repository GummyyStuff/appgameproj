# Case Opening Game - User Guide

## Overview

Case opening is an exciting game where you purchase cases containing random Tarkov-themed items. Each case has different odds and potential rewards. This guide explains how the game works, why it's fair, and what you can expect.

## How It Works

### Basic Gameplay

1. **Select a Case**: Choose from available case types (Scav, PMC, or Labs)
2. **Purchase**: Spend your virtual currency (roubles) to open the case
3. **Watch the Animation**: Enjoy the opening animation as items scroll by
4. **Receive Your Prize**: Get a random item based on the case's drop rates
5. **Auto-Convert**: Your item value is automatically added to your balance

### What Makes It Fair?

We use **provably fair** algorithms to ensure every opening is genuinely random:

- **Cryptographically Secure Random Generation**: Uses cryptographic randomness that cannot be predicted
- **Server-Side Logic**: All calculations happen on secure servers
- **Transparent Drop Rates**: Every case clearly displays its drop rates
- **Independent Events**: Each opening is completely independent - past results don't affect future ones
- **Auditable**: All opening results can be verified for fairness

## Fairness & Transparency

### How We Ensure Fairness

**Cryptographically Secure**: We use industry-standard cryptographic random number generation that cannot be predicted or manipulated.

**Server-Side**: All randomness and calculations happen on secure servers, not in your browser.

**Transparent Rates**: Every case type clearly displays its exact drop rates for each rarity tier.

**Independent Events**: Each opening is completely independent - opening 100 cases doesn't guarantee a legendary on the 101st.

**No Patterns**: There are no patterns, "hot streaks," or timing strategies. Every opening is pure chance.

### The Math Behind It

The game uses a **two-step random selection**:

1. **Step 1**: Randomly selects a rarity tier based on the case's distribution table
2. **Step 2**: Randomly selects an item from that rarity tier

This ensures:
- Drop rates are respected across millions of openings
- Items within each rarity are equally likely
- No one item dominates despite rarity

### House Edge Explained

The "house edge" is the difference between what you pay and what you get back on average:

- **Starter Case**: 6% edge = On average, you lose 29₽ per case
- **Military Case**: 4% edge = On average, you lose 46₽ per case
- **Premium Case**: 5% edge = On average, you lose 122₽ per case
- **Legendary Case**: 2% edge = On average, you lose 106₽ per case

This edge ensures the game economy remains stable and the platform can operate.

## Strategy & Tips

### For Beginners

1. **Start with Starter Cases**: Lower risk, learn how the system works
2. **Set a Budget**: Decide how much you're willing to spend before starting
3. **View Contents**: Check what items can drop from each case
4. **Track Your Results**: Monitor your win/loss ratio

### Advanced Strategies

**Case Strategy Guide**:
- **Starter Case** (500₽): 94% return rate, safest option with mostly common/uncommon items
- **Military Case** (1,200₽): 96% return rate, best overall value with balanced uncommon/rare items
- **Premium Case** (2,500₽): 95% return rate, 15% epic chance with no common tier items
- **Legendary Case** (5,500₽): 98% return rate, 5% legendary chance, best long-term returns but highest investment

**Bankroll Management**: 
- Never risk more than 10% of your balance per session
- Start with Starter cases to learn the ropes
- Graduate to Military/Premium for better odds
- Save Legendary cases for when you have the bankroll

**Diversification**: Mix different case types based on your balance and risk tolerance

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Gambler's Fallacy**: Thinking "I'm due for a win" after losses - each opening is independent
2. **Chasing Losses**: Opening more cases to recover losses often makes it worse
3. **Ignoring Odds**: Not understanding that rare items are, well, rare
4. **Emotional Decisions**: Opening cases based on feelings rather than strategy
5. **Overspending**: Betting more than you can afford to lose

## FAQ

**Q: Are the drop rates honest?**
A: Yes. All drop rates are transparent and use cryptographically secure random generation. We can't manipulate outcomes.

**Q: Can I predict what I'll get?**
A: No. Each opening is completely random and independent. There's no way to predict or influence the result.

**Q: Should I open expensive or cheap cases?**
A: It depends on your risk tolerance. Starter cases are safest but no chance for legendaries. Legendary cases offer the best rewards but highest risk.

**Q: What if I disconnect during opening?**
A: Don't worry - the opening completes automatically on the server. Your result is saved and will be displayed when you return.

**Q: Can I trade items with other players?**
A: No, items are automatically converted to currency. However, you can share your results with friends.

**Q: Is there a "best time" to open cases?**
A: No. Each opening is independent and random regardless of timing.

**Q: How often can I open cases?**
A: As often as your balance allows. There are no daily limits, but rate limiting may apply to prevent abuse.

**Q: What happens to my balance?**
A: When you open a case, the cost is deducted immediately. When you receive an item, its value is added to your balance.

**Q: Can I get my money back?**
A: The game uses virtual currency with no real-world value. All transactions are final.

**Q: How do I see my opening history?**
A: View your recent case openings in the history panel. This shows all items you've received and their values.
